# DragonMineZ 2.2.1 — Hotfix 23

- Added JSON-defined energy avatars with configurable appearance, lifetime, costs and optional attack/effect pulses.
- Projections follow their caster, respect walls and native friendly/PvP protections, and enforce per-caster limits without forcing chunk generation.
- JSON/Lua gameplay actions can request creation; transient avatars expire and are never stored in world saves.
