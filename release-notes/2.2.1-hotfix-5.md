# DragonMineZ 2.2.1 — Hotfix 5

- Move search-flight movement and rotation to the server, with client prediction and correction.
- Add full pitch, yaw and roll controls inspired by Do a Barrel Roll: mouse steering, keyboard yaw, inertia and banking.
- Rotate the physical body with the player; use form-scaled hitboxes for obstacle collision, melee, Ki beams and projectiles.
- Align flight eye/camera positions and remote body poses; preserve teleports and combat knockback.
- Keep client/server entity caches separate in singleplayer, even when entity IDs match.
- Expose server-owned flight settings in the synchronized runtime JSON. Existing combat and scripted strike-flight handlers remain available.
