# DragonMineZ 2.2.1 — Hotfix 24

- Rocky now follows native TerraBlender climate slots and terrain surfaces, with common biome tags and configurable region weight/anchors.
- Added generic JSON noise-source bindings so dimensions can share active modded terrain or climate while keeping their own materials and features.
- Namek follows active Overworld terrain/climate; Sacred Kai shares climate while retaining its special terrain. Layered dimensions retain their generation.
- Removed duplicate canyon passes. Existing chunks and saved generator definitions are preserved; terrain-setting changes apply to newly generated terrain.
