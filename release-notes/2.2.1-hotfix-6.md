- Add conditional flight profiles in `progression/runtime.json`, including selection by active form, content, mastery and declared meters.
- Configure pitch, yaw and roll rates separately. Missing values inherit the base settings; the first matching profile wins.
- Preserve base tuning when conditions stop matching, without leaking settings or costs from the previous profile.
- Predict energy-dependent motion restrictions and retain them during input replay.
- Calculate extra flight Ki drain from the boost actually applied by the server, rather than the vanilla sprint flag.
- Require matching updated clients for the new flight prediction/config semantics. Existing JSON configVersion values remain unchanged.
